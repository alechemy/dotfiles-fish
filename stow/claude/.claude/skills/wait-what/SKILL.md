Wait, I don't understand where you've got to here.

Re-pitch that. Give me a little bit of context first, then talk in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English: one idea per sentence, active voice, the plain word over the technical one, no clause stacking.

Use the project's own vocabulary for domain terms rather than inventing new ones. If the repo has a `CONTEXT.md` or a glossary, take the terms from there (follow `CONTEXT-MAP.md` to the right one if there is more than one); if it doesn't, take them from the code's own naming.

Don't restate the same explanation more slowly. Find the thing I actually don't have and lead with that.
